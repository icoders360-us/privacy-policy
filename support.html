Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account?

No. The App works fully without sign-in — your progress, bookmarks, and settings are stored only on your device.

What's included for free?

The first two question sets are free to practice, along with one free attempt at the random "Prüfung starten" exam. Additional sets, unlimited random exams, and the AI "Explain More" chat are unlocked with a one-time purchase (EB Premium).

I made a purchase but it's not unlocking content — what do I do?

Try restoring your purchase from the App's settings screen. If that doesn't resolve it, email us at the address above with your purchase confirmation and we'll help sort it out.

How do I reset my progress?

Progress, bookmarks, and preferences can be managed from the App's settings screen. If you need help resetting something specific, contact us.
